# Hi, I'm Eren 👋

I'm a **Unity Game Developer & Game Designer** based in Istanbul, focused on **player-facing UI, game feel, and FMOD audio**.

Before moving into professional production, I spent four years reviewing and breaking down indie games on YouTube—looking closely at why certain mechanics, UI layouts, and moment-to-moment feedback loops immediately click in a player's hands while others get in the way. Today, that player-first perspective drives how I build games in Unity.

Whether I'm designing an interaction flow, tuning layered audio cues in FMOD, or hooking up keyboard and gamepad navigation, my main goal is simple: **make every click, menu transition, and gameplay action feel clear, responsive, and satisfying—even after hours of repeated play.**

## 🎮 What I'm Working On

### **Unity Developer @ BioTech Gameworks** *(Oct 2026 – Present)*
I'm currently working on a **Windows PC idle blacksmith game**, focusing on how the workshop feels, sounds, and responds to the player:
- **Player-Facing UI & Interaction Flows:** Building clean, readable interfaces and smooth interaction loops for forging, inventory management, and commerce.
- **FMOD Audio & Soundscape:** Integrating and tuning event-driven audio across workshop, crafting, item, and UI interactions—using layered material cues and ambient loops designed so repeated actions stay tactile and rewarding without causing listener fatigue.
- **Game Feel & Accessibility:** Polishing moment-to-moment audio-visual feedback and building localized settings UI with persistent master, music, and SFX controls.

### **Unity Developer @ Bosphorus Games** *(Sep 2025 – Oct 2026 | Intern → Full-Time)*
I worked on **[Gemmy Gems: A Cozy Shop Sim](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4103850/Gemmy_Gems_A_Cozy_Shop_Sim/)** for over a year, moving to full-time in August 2026 and continuing to support the team's **Steam Next Fest** build alongside my transition to BioTech Gameworks:
- **UI, Input & Feedback:** Built the player-facing UI and input flows for land selection, mining, and workshop certification with seamless mouse, keyboard, and gamepad navigation, paired with FMOD audio, VFX feedback, and English/Turkish localization.
- **Designer-Friendly Editor Tools:** Created custom Unity Editor windows, inspectors, and `ScriptableObject` workflows for land and parcel authoring, inspection, and live preview—helping the team test and iterate on the player experience directly inside Unity without waiting on code changes.
